Harvest Now vs Waiting

Count is the market-size basis. ABW is linked automatically: ABW (g) = 1000 ÷ count (pcs/kg). Both Count and ABW remain editable for every future scenario.

ScenarioCount

How to use: Enter the market price for each expected count. For 7/14/21 days, edit either Count or ABW — the other value updates automatically. Count is always rounded to a whole number using normal half-up rounding (e.g. 40.49 → 40, 40.50 → 41). Projected P/L includes the pond's cost to date plus estimated future incremental feed cost. Profit % is calculated as projected P/L ÷ total projected pond cost × 100. Negative is red, 0–20% is yellow, and above 20% is green. “Change vs Now” isolates the financial benefit of waiting.
